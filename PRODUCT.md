## Product Principles
1. **Truth Over Hype**: Every skill, technology, and metric claimed is backed by verifiable code, working sandboxes, or documented credentials.
2. **Dual-Lens Design**: Serve both rapid executive scanning (Recruiter Dossier) and rich creative expression (Social Studio) with equal craft and purpose.
3. **Frictionless Proof**: Enable recruiters to test architectural mechanisms (offline queues, tax calculations, API reviews) instantly in the browser.
4. **Performance & Polish**: Maintain rapid load times, responsive fluid layouts across mobile and desktop, and crisp typographic hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Fully responsive layout spanning mobile viewports up to wide screens (`max-w-[1560px]`).
- Semantic HTML landmarks (`main`, `nav`, `aside`), ARIA accessibility on interactive components (3D canvas, story viewer, tabs), and high contrast color palette support in both dark and light modes.
